# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Zero-config peer discovery over mDNS.
- Approved, end-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer connections with key fingerprints.
- Direct messages and hosted group chats (public and invite-only).
- Workspace sharing with live co-editing (Yjs), remote cursors, and per-member edit access.
- Group and 1:1 voice calls over WebRTC, with mute and speaking indicators.
